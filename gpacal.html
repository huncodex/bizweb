How to Calculate Your University GPA Online?

Keeping track of your Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) is highly essential for higher education and career advancements. Our free University GPA Calculator Sri Lanka provides an easy-to-use interactive interface tailored for state and private universities including University of Colombo, University of Sri Jayewardenepura, University of Kelaniya, and more.

Steps to Use the Online GPA Tracker:

  • Select your current academic academic year tab (Year 1 to Year 4).
  • Enter your Subject Code (The last digit will automatically determine the credit value).
  • Choose the correct Grade obtained from the dropdown window.
  • View your automated live dashboard summary and export the official layout as a clean PDF document.

Why Is Keeping Track of Credits Important?

Every course module carries a designated weight known as academic credits. Your final grade points are multiplied by these values to determine the final weighted average score. This tool automates the process and eliminates standard mathematical calculation errors instantly.
